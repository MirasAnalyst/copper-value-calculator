Chalcopyrite holds most of the world's copper, and it resists leaching.

Vanadium(II) reduces it to chalcocite at room temperature. A ferric leach then dissolves the copper, and SX-EW plates cathode. An electrolyzer recharges the vanadium, so electricity, not heat, drives the process.
